EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
This document defines a Communication and Dissemination plan for the AVANT project, with the objective to maximize the 
project's impact and success through targeted and effective strategies. 
The deliverable, annexed to document Quality Management Plan, will be continuously updated with the next issues 
scheduled for M18, M36, and M50 of the project’s lifespan. 
The Dissemination and Communication Plan is structured into 4 main sections 
• 
Section 1 provides an introduction to the activities described in the Dissemination and Communication plan and 
explains the context in which AVANT operates; this section is provided in chapter 3 Introduction; 
• 
Section 2 describes the Dissemination and Communication Strategy detailing about main goals, target 
audiences and related key messages to be conveyed; this section is provided in chapter 4 Communication & 
Dissemination strategy; 
• 
Section 3 focuses on the implementation of the strategy and the definition of a plan organized into 2 strands of 
activities: Marketing & Communication and Dissemination & Press. This section concretely explains how 
messages are delivered, and which tools are made available to optimise external communication; this part is 
provided in chapter 5 Communication and Dissemination plan; 
• 
Section 4 concludes the document with some final considerations and next steps.

INTRODUCTION 
The plan focuses on two main activities: Marketing&Communication (M&C) and Dissemination&Press (D&P). These activities 
are crucial for the promotion and dissemination of the AVANT project, ensuring maximum visibility and impact among 
various stakeholders. 
Marketing & Communication Activities 
The M&C activity is designed to create awareness and interest in the AVANT project. This will be achieved through a series of 
targeted activities, including: 
- 
AVANT Branding: Developing the AVANT logo and consistent branding elements. 
- 
Digital Presence, Website and social media campaigns: these activities focus on reaching a broad audience, raising 
awareness about the project. 
- 
Email marketing: the AVANT story will be narrated through periodic newsletters, updating recipients on project 
progress, upcoming events, and achieved results. 
- 
Information Material: Producing templates and information content as necessary (printable - banners, posters, flyers; 
and multimedia - videos, digital flyers and infographics). 
- 
AVANT Information Kit: designed to provide a comprehensive and cohesive set of materials that effectively 
communicate all aspects of the AVANT project. This kit will serve as a go-to resource for anyone needing detailed 
information about the project, ensuring consistent and accurate messaging. 
- 
Awareness and Engagement Campaigns: Launching campaigns to build engagement and cooperation with the 
project (this activity will be in support to specific project collaboration activities). 
Dissemination and Press Activities 
D&P are fundamental to sharing the results of the AVANT project focusing on general public but also interested scientific and 
professional communities with the aim of making the AVANT research and innovation outputs available for use and uptake 
by specific audiences identified. The planned actions include: 
- 
Publications and Press Releases: Drafting and distributing articles, press releases and other outreach materials to 
newspapers and trade magazines to announce important milestones and relevant news. 
- 
Events, Conferences and Workshops: Participating in and organizing events, conferences, and workshops to present 
the project, share knowledge, and foster networking. 
- 
Scientific Papers: Submitting articles and studies to peer-reviewed scientific journals to ensure the project's validity 
and academic recognition. 
 
Objectives 
The overall activities described in the Communication and Dissemination plan for the AVANT project aim to: 
- 
Increase the visibility and recognition of the project at national and international levels. 
- 
Promote the adoption, impact and implementation of the innovations developed within the project. 
- 
Create a solid network of stakeholders interested in the project's success. 
- 
Ensure transparent and continuous communication with the target audiences and interested communities.

COMMUNICATION & DISSEMINATION STRATEGY 
To give strength and consistency to the messages disseminated, the project implements a Communication & Dissemination 
strategy, which identifies the projects dissemination and communication channels on which the strategy will be rolled out. 
The ultimate goal of the AVANT Communication and Dissemination strategy is to promote awareness of the project as a 
whole and to facilitate the adoption of the project’s research and innovation outputs among key stakeholders, including 
academia, SMEs, industry, and other relevant players. 
The key focus of the strategy is two-fold: 
- 
Designing clear and impactful communication and dissemination objectives that align with the overall goals of the 
AVANT project. 
- 
Identifying and establishing the target audiences on a European and global scale (scientific community, end users, 
policymakers, industry, wider community, etc.), and their role in contributing to the maximization of AVANT's impact 
as well as the key messages to be conveyed. 
To ensure consistency, quality, and compliance with Engineering corporate guidelines in the communication activities of the 
IPCEI-AVANT project, a specific procedure for the production and/or validation of content must be activated. Specifically, 
prior to any institutional communication and dissemination activity (e.g., participation in events, writing and publishing 
articles, press releases, outreach material), it is necessary to inform and obtain consent from the appropriate corporate 
structure, namely the AVANT Communication Manager (Grant Office). This individual and his/her team will serve as the 
primary interface with the internal Communications & Public Affairs and Marketing departments within Engineering. 
The AVANT Communication team centralizes the information related to the overall IPCEI-AVANT communication, both 
general-purpose and technical/scientific. However, its involvement varies across different phases of the communication cycle. 
Specifically, the AVANT Communication team will take on a promotive role in Phase 1 – Awareness, a supportive role in Phase 
2 – Visibility, which focuses more on technical/scientific dissemination. A dedicated project-specific role will lead activities 
dedicated to Phase 3 – Engagement.  
However, in all three phases, the AVANT Communication team works to ensure consistency and quality in the content 
produced.

AVANT COMMUNICATION & DISSEMINATION GOALS 
The overall goal is to raise AVANT as a key player in the evolution of the next generation cloud infrastructure & services.  
In this context, AVANT is positioned as a 
- 
Catalyst for Change - an actor whose activities can drive the shift towards the concept of digital twins as a service 
and cognitive cloud. 
- 
Collaborative Hub: seeking active contributions from a wide range of sources and fostering collaboration. 
The Communication and Dissemination activities in AVANT stand on three goals: 
- 
Phase 1 - Building awareness and ensuring that AVANT becomes widely known and recognized.; 
- 
Phase 2 – Providing visibility to AVANT disseminating its results and positioning it as beneficial to business, research, 
and academia; 
- 
Phase 3 – Engaging stakeholders in AVANT Community, generating active interest and encouraging adoption.

TARGET AUDIENCES AND KEY MESSAGES 
The overall IPCEI program encourages cross-border cooperation, bringing together a diverse range of stakeholders, including 
governments, private companies, and research institutions. This approach underscores the EU's commitment to collaborative 
innovation and shared economic prosperity, tackling large-scale projects that no single Member State could achieve alone, 
thereby strengthening the EU's position in the global economy. In order to achieve AVANT desired impacts, the plan 
identifies a number of target audiences as well as specific key messages to be conveyed. 
4.2.1 
POLICY MAKERS, MIMIT, EC, EU INSTITUTIONS 
Goal: Communicate the alignment and support of AVANT with European and National policies. 
Key Messages: 
- 
Demonstrate how AVANT supports and aligns with European and national policies. 
- 
Engage with policymakers to ensure AVANT contributes to policies under development, particularly focusing on 
cloud to edge technologies. 
- 
Position AVANT as a pivotal experience for shaping future policy evolutions.

linked to business interests. 
4.2.3 
RESEARCH AND OTHER IPCEI PROJECTS 
Other IPCEI projects are also a key target audience since they, together with AVANT, aim to creating positive spillover effects 
for the entire community. In AVANT, two types of collaborations with IPCEI partner companies will be considered: direct 
partnerships, involving formal agreements to work on specific aspects of the project, sharing knowledge, technology, and 
resources; and indirect partnerships, which might include less formal collaborations with entities within the broader IPCEI 
network, potentially offering complementary expertise or resources. 
Key Messages: 
- 
Inform about the valuable improvements and advancements of existing technologies facilitated by AVANT. 
- 
Highlight the collaborative potential and synergistic benefits of engaging with AVANT. 
- 
Showcase AVANT's contributions to the broader IPCEI objectives, fostering a sense of shared purpose and mutual 
benefit.

5.2.1 
PRESS RELEASES 
During the project lifetime, several press releases will be produced. Specifically, all press releases issued must be coordinated 
by the AVANT Communication team and will be delivered by the Engineering Communication and Public Affairs Dept. 
Press releases can be proposed by any member of AVANT, R&I and BU, by sending a request to the AVANT Communication

The ideal process to start working on a press release is to   
- 
AVANT members send an email to AVANT Communication Manager, about the topic they would like to address;  
- 
once the formal approval has been received, they can provide an initial draft that should be uploaded in the 
document library portal;  
- 
the process continues with interactions between the member proposing the press release until the final version is 
produced;  
- 
the press release is then shared with the Communication and Public Affairs dept who will work on the final shape

5.2.2 
PUBLICATIONS 
If one or more project’s member intends to submit for publication a part of work performed within the project, the member 
shall inform the Executive Board and the AVANT Communication team at least 30 days before the submission, by sending an 
email to them with the relevant information about that publication (titles, authors, abstract).   
Any objection to the planned publication shall be made within 20 calendar days after receipt of the notice. If no objection is 
made within the time limit stated above, the publication is permitted. Examples of justified objections are: (a) academic or 
commercial interests compromised by the publication; (b) legal, privacy, ethical constraints not respected; (c). Other 
objections could be justified. However, any objection has to include a precise request for necessary modifications. If an 
objection has been raised, the involved partners shall discuss how to overcome the justified grounds for the objection on a 
timely basis (for example by amending the planned publication and/or by protecting information before publication). A 
written acceptance shall be returned to the AVANT member (within 2 weeks) before he/she proceeds to the submission. 
Moreover, the participation in exhibitions through a stand and the presentation of demos of the project results require prior 
agreement of the whole project Consortium.   
All publications, presentations, active participation to events etc. in the name of the project must be announced to the AVANT 
Director and Executive Board and Communication Manager and will be centrally documented. Updates need to be done in 
the respective tables in the interim reports.
