Introduction to IPCEIs and IPCEI CIS 
Important Projects of Common European Interest (IPCEIs) represent a key strategy of the European Union to 
promote innovation, growth and competitiveness among its Member States. IPCEIs support projects of great 
strategic importance that address transnational challenges and contribute significantly to the EU's economic, 
social and environmental objectives. 
The European Commission Decision C(2023) 8552 final of 5 December 2023 authorized state aid from seven 
countries (France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Poland, Spain and Hungary) to support the first IPCEI dedicated to 
technologies for the creation of a European value chain in Next Generation Cloud Infrastructure and Services, 
called "IPCEI CIS"1. 
Italy is participating in IPCEI CIS with 5 companies - Engineering, Fincantieri, Reply, Tim, Tiscali Italia - and 2 
research centers - Enea and Bruno Kessler Foundation - playing a leading role in the cloud infrastructure and 
services sector thanks to the investments programmed through the project. The Commission has authorized Italy 
to grant a maximum of approximately €409 million in state aid to the 5 participating Italian companies, out of a 
total of over €1.2 billion at European level. 
Engineering is participating in IPCEI CIS with the AVANT project and is part of the Facilitation Group, the 
technical-administrative governing body of IPCEI to support the Supervisory Board, within which Italy is also 
represented.

2.1 
THE ENGINEERING AVANT PROJECT 
AVANT is the Engineering project conceived within the IPCEI CIS framework. AVANT aims to develop Research, 
Development and Innovation (R&D&I) activities aimed at providing advanced solutions for the creation and 
operation of complex systems based on Digital Twins.

In an era characterized by increasing interconnection and distribution of software systems, it is essential to 
develop technological infrastructures that can quickly adapt to changing user needs and guarantee efficient and 
sustainable operation.  
AVANT fits into this context with a twofold objective: from a technological point of view, to support the 
transition from centralized clouds to distributed, heterogeneous and "cognitive" clouds, capable of evolving 
dynamically, responding to the requests of a wide range of applications and sectors; from a business point of 
view, to fill the gaps in a highly fragmented European market, with evident coordination problems between the 
major European players. To achieve these objectives, AVANT proposes the creation of an integrated platform 
that reduces the time required to deploy advanced resources for data analysis and forecasting the evolution of 
digital twins.

2.3 
PROBLEMS SOLVED THANKS TO TECHNOLOGY: CLOUD AND DIGITAL TWIN 
Through the use of advanced technologies, such as distributed machine learning and digital twins, AVANT 
addresses two main market needs: 
• 
Reproducibility and Scalability of Digital Twins: despite the growing importance of digital twins, their 
engineering is not yet sufficiently developed to be reproduced reliably in predefined and limited 
contexts. AVANT aims to make digital twins available "as a service", facilitating their integration in 
various sectors. 
• 
Adaptability of ICT Infrastructures: by creating a "cognitive" cloud, AVANT intends to develop an ICT 
infrastructure that is able to quickly adapt to the needs of complex and interconnected systems, 
improving the overall sustainability of the sector. 
AVANT is focused on Digital Twins;  aDigital Twin (DT) is the digital counterpart of a physical object or process. 
By leveraging a mix of possibly real-time data, simulation algorithms, and AI models, DTs can substantially raise 
the efficiency and effectiveness of real processes, improving economic and environmental performance.  
Most of DT-related technologies are still proprietary, fragmented, complex, hard to maintain -> expensive (TCO).

3.1 
COMMITTEES  
3.1.1 
PROJECT BOARD (PB) 
Members of the Project Board (PB) are the Project Manager (PM), the Product Manager (PRM), the Technical 
Manager (TM), the Privacy & Ethics Officer (PEO), the Security Officer (SO), the WP Coordinators (WPC), and the 
Project Management Office Whenever financial issues should raise the CFO will be invited for having its support 
on how to carry on the activities  
Project Board meets at least once every month or upon request of any member of the PB. The PB is the executive 
body of the Project, in charge of the overall coordination, progress monitoring, and security/ethics issues. The PB 
has the responsibility to monitor ethical compliance.  
3.1.2 
TECHNICAL MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE (TMC) 
The Technical Management Committee (TMC) is led by the Technical Manager (TM) and the Project Manager 
(PM), and includes all WP Techinical Leader sand Coordinators and Task Leaders. 
The TMC meets at least once every month, or upon request of any member of the TMC.  
The TMC monitors progress of the AVANT project and the quality of Avant results, managing the day-to-day 
activity of the WPs. Among its tasks: the approval of the AVANT architecture; defining responsibilities for 
software components; setting the detailed scope for technical work; ensuring technical consistency among WPs, 
between the use case/requirements and technical WPs; assess risks to Project impacts.   
3.1.3 
IPCEI GOVERNANCE 
IPCEI is a large project, composed by many different project funded by different member states. As such a 
governance body as been established. The IPCEI governance relies on the interactions among a “Facilitation 
group”, a “supervisory board”   
3.1.4 
HIGHER MANAGEMENT  
Higher management, such as the Vice President (VP) or Chief Executive Officer (CEO), plays a crucial role in the 
decision-making process, primarily focusing on strategic guidance and overarching governance. Here's a detailed 
look at their key responsibilities: 
1. Strategic Vision and Alignment: 
• 
Higher management sets the overall strategic vision for the project, ensuring alignment with the 
company's long-term goals and objectives. They provide direction, outlining the project's 
significance to the organization's mission and growth strategy. 
2. Governance and Oversight: 
• 
VPs and CEOs oversee the project's progress, ensuring adherence to governance standards. They 
establish frameworks for compliance, risk management, and ethical conduct, guaranteeing that 
the project adheres to relevant laws and regulations. 
3. Resource Allocation: 
• 
Higher management decides on the allocation of resources, including budgetary allocations, 
personnel, and other assets necessary for successful project execution. They ensure that 
resources are distributed efficiently, supporting both immediate project needs and long-term 
goals.

4. Stakeholder Management: 
• 
Higher management plays a vital role in managing key stakeholders, including investors, clients, 
and partners. They engage with stakeholders, communicating project progress, milestones, and 
challenges, thereby building confidence and maintaining support. 
5. Decision-Making on Critical Issues: 
• 
In cases of critical decisions that affect the project's direction, scope, or viability, higher 
management steps in to provide resolutions. They handle major issues that might have 
significant ramifications, such as project pivots, major acquisitions, or changes in leadership. 
6. Approval of Major Milestones: 
• 
VPs and CEOs approve significant project milestones, such as phase completions, go/no-go 
decisions, or project launches. Their approval signifies the project's readiness to proceed to the 
next stage or reach completion. 
7. Crisis Management: 
• 
Higher management is involved in managing project crises, ensuring that appropriate measures 
are taken to address and resolve emergencies. They guide the project team through significant 
challenges, minimizing potential disruptions. 
In conclusion, higher management, including VPs and CEOs, plays a fundamental role in providing strategic 
leadership, governance, and oversight for large projects. Their involvement ensures that projects stay aligned 
with the organization's goals, resources are appropriately allocated, and critical decisions are made effectively, 
supporting successful project execution. 
 
3.2 
ROLES 
3.2.1 
PROJECT DIRECTOR 
A Project Director is the professional responsible for the overall planning, execution, and delivery of a project, 
ensuring it is completed within the constraints of time, budget, and quality. 
Their primary responsibilities include: 
• 
Defining project vision and goals: The Project Director collaborates with stakeholders to clearly define 
project objectives and ensure alignment with the organization's strategy. 
• 
Planning the project: The Project Director develops a detailed project plan that outlines the tasks, 
timelines, resources, and costs required to complete the project. 
• 
Managing the project team: The Project Director leads and motivates the project team, assigning tasks, 
delegating responsibilities, and monitoring their performance. 
• 
Monitoring and controlling project progress: The Project Director regularly monitors project progress 
against the plan and identifies and resolves any issues or risks that could impede project success. 
• 
Communicating with stakeholders: The Project Director maintains open and transparent 
communication with all project stakeholders, providing regular updates on project status and gathering 
feedback. 
• 
Managing risks: The Project Director identifies potential risks that could impact the project and 
develops mitigation plans to minimize their impact. 
• 
Negotiating and managing contracts: The Project Director manages contracts with vendors and 
contractors, ensuring work is completed according to agreed-upon specifications and within budget. 
• 
Managing project changes: The Project Director effectively manages project changes, assessing their 
impact on the plan and budget and obtaining necessary approvals.

WORKPACKEGE WP3  
WP3 has three main innovation objectives: 
• 
Firstly, it aims to study and understand potential cyberattacks and incidents that can occur in DT 
through the cloud continuum. 
• 
Secondly, it aims to provide specific cybersecurity measures for DT service delivery platforms that 
enable reliable, secure, and resilient DT operations. 
• 
Thirdly, it aims to explore the potential of DT to become a single digital collaboration space for 
protecting against cyberattacks. 
The reassessment of WP3 activities, carried out at the beginning of the AVANT project, revealed the 
structural dependence that the above-mentioned objectives have on other AVANT WPs, particularly 
those focused on infrastructure and data service levels (i.e., WP1 and WP2). While it is true that security 
must go hand in hand with architectural decisions and the choice of technologies, it is also true that 
security in itself has little meaning when other work packages are still in a very early stage. 
For these reasons, it was deemed appropriate to reschedule the start of WP3 activities to early 2024, 
when stable inputs from other WPs will be available. Considering the initial phase of the project, this 
rescheduling will not have a significant impact on the publication of WP3 results, nor on the security of 
other results published by AVANT. On the contrary, it allows WP3 work to focus better on AVANT-
specific solutions and technologies, thus avoiding the dispersion of efforts in directions that are not of 
interest to the project 
WORKPACKEGE WP4 
In order to optimize workflow and ensure greater flexibility, it was decided to bring forward Tasks 4.1 and 
4.2, initially scheduled for the end of the first and third quarters, respectively. This decision, implemented 
from the first month of the project, was motivated by the need to align the activities of WP4 with the 
timelines of the other Work Packages and to ensure optimal synergy between the various phases of the 
project. The advancement of these tasks has allowed for the timely initiation of preliminary analyses and 
research, fundamental to subsequent activities, and has minimized the risk of delays or overlaps.

5.1 
QUALITY ASSURANCE AND RISK MANAGEMENT 
The IPCEI Avant project is built upon two fundamental pillars: Quality Assurance (QA) and Risk Management. 
The quality plan outlines the standards and procedures necessary to achieve project excellence. By integrating 
the collaboration plan and the communication plan, the quality plan ensures that all stakeholders are actively 
involved in verification and evaluation processes, contributing to a constant alignment of objectives and 
activities. 
The risk plan, accompanied by a detailed and continuously updated risk register, plays a crucial role in proactively 
identifying, assessing, and mitigating potential threats that could jeopardize the project's success. Through in-
depth risk analysis, the plan enables the definition of specific preventive and corrective actions to reduce the 
probability and impact of negative events. 
This integrated approach, encompassing all aspects of the project, ensures that IPCEI Avant remains focused on 
its predefined objectives and delivers high-quality results. Moreover, it fosters effective collaboration among 
partners, strengthening the sense of cohesion and shared responsibility. 
5.2 
ETHICS AND PRIVACY MANAGEMENT 
This project's Ethics and Privacy Management Plan is a strategic instrument that will govern the handling of 
personal data. It will ensure adherence to relevant regulations and foster a corporate culture rooted in 
transparency, responsibility, and respect for individual privacy. 
5.3 
DNSH - COMPLIANCE WITH THE DO NO SIGNIFICANT HARM PRINCIPLES 
Adherence to the DNSH principle is a distinguishing feature of the IPCEI Avant, demonstrating the project's 
commitment to combining innovation and sustainability. This approach not only guarantees a more sustainable 
future for our planet but also opens up new opportunities for economic growth and technological development. 
These principles and commitments have been central to the project's design. The project aims to develop 
innovative technological solutions to optimize the use of energy resources and reduce the environmental impact 
of information and communication technologies. Through the application of artificial intelligence techniques, the 
adoption of distributed computing architectures, and the implementation of efficient communication protocols, 
the project aims to achieve significant energy savings. In particular, it will focus on developing platforms for data 
processing at the edge and creating digital twins for the simulation and optimization of industrial processes. 
In line with our commitment to sustainability, a report will be produced in the coming months (M36) 
demonstrating full compliance with the DNSH principles.
