PRODUCT MANAGEMENT PLAN 
1.1 
OBJECTIVES  
The Product Management Plan outlines the strategic approach and operational framework for the ideation, development, 
launch, and continuous improvement of AVANT Product. 
This document serves as a comprehensive guide for all stakeholders involved in the product lifecycle, ensuring alignment with 
the company’s vision, market demands, and customer needs. This plan aims to facilitate effective communication, streamline 
workflows, and enhance decision-making throughout the product’s journey from conception to market success. 
The plan aims to:  
 
Align Stakeholders: Ensure all team members and stakeholders have a unified understanding of the product’s 
purpose, target market, and key features. 
 
Align Research and Innovation trends: Integrate the latest research and innovation trends to keep the product 
competitive and forward-thinking. 
 
Guide Development: Provide a detailed framework for the product development process, including timelines, 
milestones, and deliverables. 
 
Enhance Communication: Foster transparent and effective communication among cross-functional teams to ensure 
seamless collaboration. 
 
Mitigate Risks: Identify potential risks and develop mitigation strategies to address challenges proactively. 
 
Measure Success: Establish key performance indicators (KPIs) to monitor progress and measure the success of the 
product against predefined goals.

PRODUCT MANAGEMENT ENVISAGED APPROACH 
The “Product Management Envisaged Approach” outlines the steps and methodologies that will be used to guide the product 
from conception to market launch and beyond. It includes phases such as market research, product development, testing, 
launch, and post-launch evaluation. 
 
In essence, the envisaged approach in product management is a comprehensive plan that ensures all aspects of product 
development are considered and managed effectively. It aims to align the product with market needs, technological 
advancements, and business goals, ensuring a structured and strategic path to success. 
 
The approach for the AVANT product is structured into two distinct macro phases to ensure a comprehensive and strategic 
approach to product development and market introduction.  
 
Years 1-3: Research, development and Innovation (RDI) Phase 
During the initial three years, the focus is on extensive research and innovation. This phase involves exploring cutting-edge 
technologies, conducting market analysis and developing prototypes. The goal is to gather valuable insights, validate 
concepts and lay a solid foundation for the product’s future development. 
 
From Year 4: First Industrial Development (FID) Phase 
Starting from the fourth year, the process transitions into the First Industrial Development Phase. This phase emphasizes 
scaling up production, refining the product based on feedback from the research phase, and preparing for market launch. It 
includes rigorous testing, quality assurance, and the establishment of manufacturing processes to ensure the product meets 
industry standards and customer expectations. 
2.1 
YEARS 1-3: RESEARCH, DEVELOPMENT AND INNOVATION (RDI) PHASE  
In the initial three years, the focus will be on RDI, which is crucial for laying a solid foundation for the future AVANT product. 
In particular, the AVANT product lays its foundations and will focus on the first three project work packages (WP1-WP2-WP3) 
which are transversal. The other WPs (from WP4 to WP8) represent vertical specializations and specific market domains, 
derived from research topics specific to R&D labs of Engineering and represent some of the possible application areas of 
Avant product.

The main objectives of the first phase of research and development are  
 
Identify market needs and opportunities (rolling phase). We will conduct comprehensive market research to 
pinpoint gaps and emerging trends. Engaging with our open-source community will provide valuable insights. The 
open-source community is a rich source of information and innovation. By actively participating in forums, 
conferences, and online discussions, we can gain insights into cutting-edge research and grassroots developments. 
Community members often experiment with new technologies, providing a practical perspective on their 
applicability and potential. 
 
Identify new technological research trends (rolling phase). In today’s fast-paced technological landscape, staying 
ahead of emerging trends is crucial for successful product development. We will regularly review academic journals, 
industry reports, and white papers to stay informed about the latest advancements in technology. This includes 
tracking innovations in fields relevant to our product, such as Digital Twin, artificial intelligence, machine learning, 
cloud-edge continuum, data analytics and cybersecurity. 
 
Collaborate with direct and indirect partners. Building strong relationships with both direct and indirect partners 
is essential for leveraging external expertise and resources. These collaborations can provide access to new 
technologies, market insights, and additional funding opportunities, enhancing our overall R&I capabilities. 
 
Collaborate with universities and research centers. Establishing partnerships with academic institutions will 
enhance our R&D efforts. These collaborations can lead to cutting-edge research and access to resources that may 
not be available in-house. Partnering with universities and research centers allows us to tap into ongoing research 
projects and access expertise in emerging technologies. These collaborations can lead to joint research initiatives, 
where we can explore new ideas and validate their feasibility in real-world applications. 
 
Develop prototypes and test new technologies. Our team will create prototypes to explore innovative solutions. 
The community can play a vital role in testing these prototypes, offering feedback that can guide further 
development. We will provide access to a digital playground to speed up the creation of new DTs and display 
AVANT results. 
 
Gathering Feedback from Users and Stakeholders and Optimization of Prototypes Based on Feedback: 
Engaging with both users and stakeholders, including our open-source community, will be essential for refining our 
prototypes. We will iterate on our prototypes, making improvements based on the feedback received. 
 
Preparation for the Transition to First Industrial Development. As we finalize our prototypes, we will begin 
planning for the next phase of development.

Several factors could allow EU IT to regain competitive ground. In July 2020, the EU Court of Justice invalidated the EU-U.S. 
Privacy Shield, a framework approved by EU and US governments for data transfers between the US and the European 
Economic Area. According to this decision, the Privacy Shield does not meet GDPR requirements, making it an unlawful basis 
for transferring personal data from the EU to the US. This decision can impact cloud adoption strategies for EU customers, 
who might seek services compliant with the EU legislative framework. 
EU IT providers are combining efforts in the Gaia-X initiative to introduce a cloud model focused on data sovereignty by 
integrating existing standards to ensure interoperability, establishing compliance/certification frameworks, and avoiding lock-
in effects. In the long term, the decision on the Privacy Shield might encourage the establishment of a European cloud 
through Gaia-X. Ultimately, Gaia-X’s mission is to stimulate the competitiveness of EU-based firms in the Platform Economy, 
where EU companies accounted for only 4% of platform companies listed on the stock market in 2019. Industry concentration 
is also on the rise, although this phenomenon is more prevalent in North America than in Europe. 
The annual global market for vertical software currently stands at around $100 billion and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 
11.2% over the next five years4.. Many companies are targeting this market: Salesforce acquired Vlocity, a start-up with 
industry-specific solutions; Oracle made multiple acquisitions of software companies serving industries such as construction, 
communications, retail, and travel. SAP launched an industry cloud to provide solutions for verticals (retail, logistics, 
education, construction). The field remains open, and Europe can compete. Many solutions are based on the traditional 
strengths of these companies (ERP, CRM, etc.). However, the extension to the edge is still lagging.

horizontal platforms built on European R&D excellence5.. This aligns with AVANT’s ambition. By producing new software to

3.1.2 
MARKET SITUATION  
In the mid to long term, the cloud market is expected to continue growing robustly. Offerings will increasingly tend to be full-
stack solutions rather than single components. The market will keep concentrating, with cloud vendors combining multiple 
solutions into flexible suites. If the market is not rebalanced, cloud providers may increase prices, potentially leading to “cloud 
repatriation,” where companies migrate back from cloud to on-premises solutions6.  
One of the positive effects of the IPCEI CIS is to foster a more competitive market landscape that should help contain prices. 
Additionally, more companies are facing technical challenges that motivate a shift to the computing continuum, which we 
view as the computing context of the next decade. Gartner forecasts significant growth in edge computing, with 50% of large 
enterprises developing edge investment plans7.

following the same approach, will offer further opportunities to create a collaborative environment with relevant stakeholders 
from whom we expect further feedback (QinU V&V procedures), requiring an additional RDI effort. 
Feedback from users – AVANT users (both developers and end-users) involved in FID are likely to interact with solutions they

requirements are expected to emerge not only due to the development methodology adopted, but also because the 
functioning of AVANT SW solutions also consists of the ability to exploit next-generation cloud-edge components (at 
HW/firmware, resource orchestration, processing, security, and apps levels) developed in other stages of the IPCEI-CIS value 
chain. The seamless integration of AVANT with developments coming from the other participants in the IPCEI is expected to 
require some R&D effort due to the innovative and interlinked nature of the developments.
